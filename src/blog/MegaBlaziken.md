# Pokémon #0130 - Mega Blaziken
![](assets/images/MegaBlaziken/Blaziken_main.png)

It's been a while since I've last participated in a collab. Several years ago it seemed like that's the only thing I do in this hobby. <br>
Now I just got confident enough to build in digital plus I have enough parts in my current studio to test and play. And here's my attempt at Blaziken. It's mega evolution at least. <br>
I actually had a WIP for regular Blaziken lying around abandoned but I got stuck on its head. Luckily, the mega evo is a bit easier to make with all these feathery bits.
![](assets/images/MegaBlaziken/Blaziken_face.png)
Not satisfied enough with the eye design yet. I think it should look more aggressive and striking. Maybe I can achieve it with rubber bands when I prepare the physical version of the build. Want to do this for the lego expo in summer.
![](assets/images/MegaBlaziken/Blaziken_back.png)
The back is a bit simple. I'm also not very happy with how messy the lower legs look but with a strict deadline I couldn't figure out a way for them to look like a bunch of fur but not messy. Oh well. I'll leave it forthe future me who will build the physical version
